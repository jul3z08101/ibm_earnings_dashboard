SEC & Big 4 Regulatory Guidance Knowledge Base

1. Equal or Greater Prominence : Item 10(e)(1)(i)(A) & C&DI 102.10

The most directly comparable GAAP measure must be presented before or alongside any non-GAAP measure with equal or greater prominence in headlines, tables, and narrative.

eCFR: 17 CFR 229.10(e)

2. Quantitative Reconciliation : Regulation G §244.100

Registrants must provide a clear quantitative schedule reconciling the non-GAAP measure to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.

eCFR: 17 CFR Part 244 (Reg G)

3. Prohibition on Per-Share Liquidity Measures : C&DI 102.05

Non-GAAP liquidity measures (e.g. Free Cash Flow) may not be presented on a per-share basis in any SEC filing or earnings materials.

SEC.gov: Non-GAAP Interpretations

4. Tailored Accounting Principles Prohibited : C&DI 100.04

Adjustments that accelerate revenue recognition or alter core GAAP measurement principles are prohibited as misleading.

Deloitte DART Non-GAAP Roadmap (October 2025)
